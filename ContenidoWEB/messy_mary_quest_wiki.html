🔰 Novice Grounds Quest

1 Messy Mary (Novice Grounds)

Meet Messy Mary at the Novice Grounds (new_1-3 85, 33).

She is struggling to keep her inventory organized and drops loot everywhere. After reading an ancient book about a special technique called Pickup, she asks for your help to gather practice materials so you can both learn the skill together!

🪶 2x Feather of Birds
🟢 10x Jellopy
🍀 5x Clover
🪵 5x Tree Root
🍻 Prontera Backup Teacher

2 Not-So-Messy Mary (Prontera Pub)

If you graduated from Novice Grounds without learning the skill, don't worry! You can find Not-So-Messy Mary inside the Prontera Pub (prt_in 179, 12 — pub entrance at prontera 205, 157).

Now stylish and organized, she offers a second chance to teach you the Pickup skill, though her material request is a bit tougher for main world adventurers:

📜 10x Animal Skin
🦗 10x Grasshopper Leg
🪶 10x Bill of Birds
✨ Skill Unlock

3 Reward: Pickup Skill

Upon turning in the required materials to Mary (in Novice Grounds or Prontera Pub), your character will instantly master the Pickup skill!

Skill Functionality:
A special utility skill that allows your character to swipe and instantly collect all dropped items and loot around your surrounding area in a single movement. Never leave valuable loot behind again!
