Biocannibalize Max Level: 5

Sp Cost: 20

Consumes Small Plant Bottle for levels 1 through 3, and Plant Bottle for levels 4 and 5. The skill now uses a "Summon Point" system with total points equal to 3 + Skill Level (up to 8 points at level 5). Allows summoning the following flora:

- Level 1: Mandragora (2 points)
- Level 2: Rafflesia (3 points)
- Level 3: Parasite (4 points)
- Level 4: Geographer (5 points)
- Level 5: Wooden Golem (6 points)

Note: You can summon any combination of plants as long as you have enough total Summon Points available (e.g., at level 5 with 8 points: 2 Mandragoras + 1 Parasite, 1 Rafflesia + 1 Geographer, or 1 Mandragora + 1 Wooden Golem).

Homunculus System Rework System

Embryo is now a usable item that allows you to choose from any of the 4 Homunculus types upon creation.

You can own 1 of each Homunculus class, up to a maximum of 4 active Homunculi. Progress and levels for each Homunculus are saved independently, allowing you to summon whichever one you need.

Homunculus status gain randomness has been significantly smoothed out (variance is much lower, while maximum potential status is balanced), ensuring Homunculus performance reflects player choices rather than RNG.

Homunculus Rebalance System

Filir: Cooldown for all skills reduced from 2 minutes to 30 seconds. Moonlight damage and hit count now scale with AGI.

Vanilmirth: Chaotic Blessings now only heals the Homunculus or the Alchemist.

Lif:
- Healing Touch: Converted into Healing Wind. Heals the Homunculus, the Alchemist, and summoned plants within a 3x3, 5x5, 7x7, 9x9, or 11x11 AoE area. Healing is half of Heal skill formula. (2s Cast Time, 4s Cooldown, SP Cost: 25 + 5 * Skill Level).
- Emergency Avoid: Increases Movement Speed for the Homunculus, the Alchemist, and mobile plants.
- Brain Surgery: SP regen bonus removed. Increases Healing Wind healing by 4% * Skill Level. Casting Healing Wind applies the "Regen" status for 12s * Skill Level.

Amistr: Passive: Every 1 point of DEF increases Base ATK by 1% (up to +80% at 80 DEF). Upon evolution, basic attacks deal 3x3 splash damage.

General Note: Ultimate Homunculus skills no longer consume Intimacy when cast. Each ultimate skill has a 5-minute cooldown.
