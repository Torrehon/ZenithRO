Soul of the Pugilist (NEW) Max Level: 1

Passive / Platinum

Halves SP cost for all combo skills. Enhances Iron Fists, Dodge, Critical Explosion, Steel Body, Combo Finish, Chain Combo, and Asura Strike:

- Iron Fists: Grants a (1% * Skill Level) chance to gain a Spirit Sphere when attacking.

- Dodge: Increases Flee gained by (1 * Skill Level).

- Critical Explosion: While active, performing combos (Chain Combo, Combo Finish) grants stacks of "Relentless" (up to a max of 10). Each stack gives +2 CRIT and +1% ASPD.

- Steel Body: Now allows using combo skills. While active, all combo skills gain increased damage ratio equal to (VIT * 2%).

- Combo Finish: Now deals area-of-effect (AoE) damage. At 10 Relentless stacks, strikes 2 hits.

- Chain Combo: Now deals area-of-effect (AoE) damage.

- Asura Strike: SP component damage ratio changed to (Consumed SP * Base Level) / 8, but adds +(VIT * Stacks * 10)% to the ratio. Has a 30-second cooldown.

Soul of the Ascetic (NEW) Max Level: 1

Passive / Platinum

Enhances Spirit Mastery, Finger Offensive, Investigate, Critical Explosion, Steel Body, Asura Strike, and Ki Explosion:

- Spirit Mastery: Increases max Spirit Spheres to 10. Adds 25% of your MATK to damage formula for Finger Offensive, Investigate, and Ki Explosion.

- Finger Offensive: Consumes only 1 Spirit Sphere per cast regardless of skill level.

- Investigate: If cast while under Blade Stop, hits 2 times.

- Critical Explosion: Increases ATK by (6 * Skill Level) and reduces Casting Time by (2% * Skill Level).

- Steel Body: Allows casting Finger Offensive, Investigate, and Ki Explosion. Adds INT to the damage ratio of these skills.

- Asura Strike: Base attack formula becomes hybrid (Total ATK + Total MATK). SP component damage ratio changed to (Consumed SP * Base Level) / 10, but adds +(INT * Spheres * 20)% to the ratio.

- Ki Explosion: Killing monsters with this skill grants 1 Spirit Sphere.
