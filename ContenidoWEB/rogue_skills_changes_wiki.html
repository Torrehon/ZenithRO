Mug Max Level: 5

Sp Cost: 15

Melee physical attack that inflicts (100% * Skill Level) damage with a (2% * Skill Level) chance to steal Zeny. DEX increases success chance by +1% for every 2 DEX points, while LUK increases stolen Zeny amount by +1% per LUK point. Attack element depends on equipped weapon.

Back Stab Max Level: 10

Sp Cost: 16

Acquires the Renewal mechanic of teleporting behind the target. Cannot be cast while in Hiding, and can miss. Damage ratio is 200% + (30% * Skill Level). Strikes 2 hits when equipped with Daggers, and 1 hit when equipped with Bows. Can deal Critical hits.

Raid Max Level: 5

Sp Cost: 20

Acquires Renewal mechanics, inflicting the SC_RAID status which increases damage taken by targets hit by Raid (+20% damage taken for normal monsters, +10% for MVP bosses). Damage ratio is 100% + (60% * Skill Level).

Strip Skills (Divest Skills) Max Level: 3

Sp Cost: 15 / 20 / 25

Forces target to unequip the corresponding equipment piece with a (5% * Skill Level) base success chance. Retains DEX difference success modifier (Caster DEX vs Target DEX).

Intimidate (Snatch) Max Level: 5

Sp Cost: 13 / 16 / 19 / 22 / 25

Increases damage ratio to 100% + (60% * Skill Level).

Graffiti Max Level: 5

Sp Cost: 15

Retains original effect, but now party members standing inside the area of effect gain +5% ATK and +5% MATK.

Duration: 60 seconds. Requires 1 Surface Paint per cast. Cooldown: 30 seconds.

Cleaner Max Level: 1

Sp Cost: 5

Cleans a graffiti off the ground, reducing Graffiti skill Cooldown by 30 seconds.

Opportunist (Gangster's Paradise) Max Level: 1

Passive

No longer requires sitting next to other Rogues. Instead, grants +5% Physical and Magical Damage per status debuff present on the target enemy (up to a maximum of +25% bonus damage).

Supported Debuffs: Blind, Silence, Bleeding, Curse, Poison, Confusion, Burning, Freezing, Electrocute, Buried, Drown, Laceration, Concussion, Marked, and all Divest/Strip statuses.

MVP Boss Status Adjustments (Max Duration: 20 seconds):
- Blind: Reduces HIT by 10%.
- Silence: Increases Casting Time by 25%.
- Bleeding: Inflicts DoT equal to 0.5% current HP.
- Curse: Halves LUK (no other penalty).
- Poison: Reduces DEF by 10%.
- Confusion: Reduces FLEE by 10%.
- Divest Weapon: Reduces ATK by 10%.
- Divest Shield: Reduces DEF by 5%.
- Divest Armor: Reduces VIT by 20%.
- Divest Helm: Reduces INT by 20%.
- Concussion: Reduces DEF by 15%.
- Marked: Reduces ASPD by 10%.

Plagiarism Max Level: 10

Sp Cost: 0

Now functions as an Active skill menu! While learned, the Rogue passively observes skills used on screen. Upon activating Plagiarism, a selection menu opens allowing you to choose 1 observed skill to copy at the learned level of Plagiarism.

Soul of the Nightblade (NEW) Max Level: 1

Passive / Platinum

Enhances Sword Mastery, Mug, Back Stab, Raid, Close Confine, and Opportunist:

- Sword Mastery: Allows using Double Attack with 1-Handed Swords at a (5% * Skill Level) proc chance. When equipped with Daggers, grants +10 CRIT.

- Mug: Can now deal Critical hits. Grants 50% HP Lifesteal on hit.

- Back Stab: With Daggers, has a 50% chance to inflict Laceration (a Neutral DoT physical status based on ATK). With Swords, has a 50% chance to inflict Concussion (-30% target DEF). With Bows, has a 50% chance to inflict Marked (-25% target Movement Speed and ASPD). Always strikes 2 hits regardless of weapon. Deals +25% bonus damage against targets with Laceration, Concussion, or Marked.

- Raid: Has a 25% chance to inflict the same status effects as Back Stab based on equipped weapon.

- Close Confine: Increases Divest / Strip success rate by +15%.

- Opportunist: Increases maximum damage bonus cap from +25% to +50%.

- Plagiarism: Unlocks Support Plagiarism, allowing you to learn a 2nd support skill (e.g., Heal, Blessing, Attention Concentrate). Also permits copying 2nd / Transcendent Job skills (such as Soul Breaker).
