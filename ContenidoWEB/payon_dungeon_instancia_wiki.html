1 Quest start

You will find Grandmother Kyung at Payon Archer Village (pay_arche 48, 137), right outside Payon Dungeon.

She will tell you the tragic story of her granddaughter Min-ji and ask for your help. To accept her task, you must be between Base Level 25 and 60. She will hand you 3 Holy Waters to cleanse Min-ji's grave in the caves.

2 Instance NPC

Once inside Payon Cave F1, head east until you find the Pulsing Gravestone (pay_dun00 163, 142).

This gravestone opens the gateway to the instance. You must be in a party and be the party leader. Make sure you carry Holy Water in your inventory, then initiate the gateway to be teleported inside.

3 The Tainted Gravestone

Once inside the instance (1@pay00 163, 142), interact with the Tainted Gravestone.

Pour the Holy Water on the stone to initiate the purification ritual and prepare your party for incoming enemies!

4 The Restless Dead

After pouring the Holy Water, you will face two waves of enemies attracted by the dark energy before the real battle begins.

First, you will fight the Corrupted Guardian (Soldier Skeleton), followed 10 seconds later by 10 Restless Corpses (Zombies). Defeat them to summon the spirit!

5 The Boss fight

Right after defeating the Restless Dead, tremors will shake the cave and the Tormented Weaver will emerge near the gravestone. Prepare yourself for a fierce battle!

Incense of Longing

Type: Accessory | Required Level: 1

The fragrance from this incense is the same Min-ji used to burn while working at her loom. Its scent seems to guide lost souls toward peace, weakening the resolve of the restless dead.

Increases physical and magical damage against Undead race monsters by 10%.
Allows the use of Signum Crucis Level 2.

⚔️ Boss Skills & Strategy

Skills: She will mainly use Dark Strike and Curse Attack. As soon as the battle begins, she will cast Wide Curse, inflicting Curse on everyone. When low on HP, she will cast Wide Soul Drain, syphoning the party's SP.

Strategy: Since it is an Undead-type monster, Holy element attacks (such as Heal, Holy Light, or Holy Smite) will deal massive damage. Keep your distance if you are a squishy class and let the tank hold aggro. Consider bringing extra Holy Water before challenging her.

6 Instance Rewards & Open World Boss

Once you've finished your task, head back to Grandmother Kyung (pay_arche 48, 137).

She will thank you, awarding 12,000 Base EXP, 10,000 Job EXP, 5,000 Zeny, and Min-ji's last ribbon: Dreamer's Ribbon.

🔁 Repeatable Open World Boss (FFA Mode)

Once the main quest is completed for the first time, you can summon the Tormented Weaver again at the Pulsing Gravestone on Payon Cave F1 (pay_dun00 163, 142) every 3 hours.

Required Offerings: 50 Skel-Bones, 50 Decayed Nails, and 1 Horrendous Mouth.
