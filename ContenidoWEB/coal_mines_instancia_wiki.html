1 Quest start

Speak to Garrick the Surveyor at the entrance of Coal Mines (mjo_dun01 52, 37).

He will tell you about a legendary Orichalcum vein hidden in the depths. To allow you to proceed, he requires you to be between Base Level 35 and 60. Your first task is to venture down to the 3rd floor (mjo_dun03) and exterminate 20 Skeleton Workers and 15 Mysts to secure the perimeter.

2 Miner Scout

Once you have completed the hunt on the 3rd floor, speak with the Miner Scout (mjo_dun03 300, 258).

The scout will confirm the area is cleared and explain that the Orichalcum vein is embedded in the northwestern walls. He will ask you to bring an Old Pick to extract a sample for Garrick.

3 Orichalcum Vein

Head to the northwestern corner of the floor and interact with the Orichalcum Vein (mjo_dun03 39, 255).

Use an Old Pick to strike the wall. You have a 30% chance to mine a pristine chunk of Orichalcum and crack open a deep passage. Note: You must be in a party. Once the wall is broken, the party leader opens the gateway to enter the instance.

4 The Inner Cavern

Upon entering the inner cavern (1@mjo), you will feel the cavern walls shake violently.

A few seconds after entering, the mineral guardian will awaken from the Orichalcum vein to defend its territory. Prepare your party for the final battle!

5 The Boss fight

The Mine Spirit has emerged from the Orichalcum vein! This powerful boss will defend the cave with ferocious earth magic and devastating tremors.

⚔️ Boss Skills & Strategy

Skills: Uses Auto Guard (Lv 3), Earth Spike (Lv 2), and Heaven's Drive (Lv 1). When low on HP (<25%), it will cast a devastating Earthquake Level 5.

Strategy: As a Formless race and Earth 2 element monster, Fire and Wind element attacks deal high damage. Be extremely cautious when its HP drops below 25% due to its Earthquake cast. Maintain distance or ensure your tank has high HP/DEF to survive the blast.

6 Instance Rewards & Open World Boss

After defeating the Mine Spirit, return to Garrick the Surveyor at the mine entrance (mjo_dun01 52, 37).

Handing over the raw Orichalcum sample will reward you with 110,000 Base EXP, 140,000 Job EXP, and the exclusive Mine Hat headgear.

🔁 Repeatable Open World Boss (FFA Mode)

Once the main quest is completed for the first time, you can summon the Mine Spirit again in the open public map of Coal Mines F3 (mjo_dun03 39, 255) every 3 hours.

Required Offerings: 10 Iron Ores, 50 Lanterns, and 5 Irons.
