1 Quest start

Head to Prontera's West Field (prt_fild05 270, 212) and talk to the Culvert Guardian.

He will inform you that novice adventurers are trapped inside the dungeon and ask you to deliver 20 Red Potions to help them (you can purchase these at the Tool Dealer right near the entrance). You must be between Base Level 15 and 60 to participate.

2 Instance NPC

Once inside Culvert 1 (prt_sewb1 195, 244), speak with the Guard Captain near the entrance to the 2nd floor.

He will offer to breach the gate and open the instance. In order to initiate this, you must be in a party and be the party leader. Once authorized, your team will enter the instance.

3 Alard's party

Inside the instance (1@sewb2 16, 26), you will find Adventurer Alard and his injured companions in desperate need of aid.

Hand him the 20 Red Potions to treat their wounds. Immediately after, a violent tremor will shake the sewer walls—the mutant boss has arrived!

4 Boss Fight

Advance a few steps north to confront the Mutant Thief Bug. This boss can be dangerous for lower-level characters, so stay alert!

A potent cockroach bait with an ultra-sticky adhesive. One touch, and they are done for. The smell is awful, but the results are deadly.

Increases physical and magical damage against Insect race monsters by 10%.
Allows the use of Envenom Level 2.

⚔️ Boss Skills & Strategy

Skills: This boss primarily uses Throw Sand to blind its target, poison attacks, and Magnum Break to strike nearby adventurers. When low on HP, it casts Increase Agility to boost its flee rate.

Strategy: In a party, spread out to prevent Throw Sand (3x3 AoE) from blinding multiple players, or keep your distance to avoid Magnum Break. Swordsman class excels here as Bash grants bonus HIT, bypassing the blind penalty.

5 Instance Rewards & Open World Boss

After defeating the boss, return to the Culvert Guardian outside the dungeon (prt_fild05 270, 212).

He will reward you with 3,000 Base EXP, 3,000 Job EXP, 2,000 Zeny, the Cleaner's Cloth headgear, and a Training Recommendation I certificate.

🔁 Repeatable Open World Boss (FFA Mode)

Once the main quest is completed for the first time, you can summon the Mutant Thief Bug at the Crack in the Wall on Culvert F2 (prt_sewb2 24, 68) every 3 hours.

Required Offerings: 50 Jellopies and 50 Worm Peelings.
