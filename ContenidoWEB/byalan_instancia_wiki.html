1 Quest start

Speak to Archeologist Dan near the entrance of Byalan Dungeon on Izlude Island (izlu2dun 114, 88).

He will explain that the archaeological team of Izlude is preparing to explore the deep underwater ruins. To allow you to proceed, he requires you to be between Base Level 50 and 65 (1st Class / High Novice only). Your first task is to venture down into Byalan Dungeon and exterminate 10 Marcs, 10 Swordfish, and 10 Phens to secure the excavation perimeter.

2 Adventurer Alard

Descend to the 3rd floor of Byalan Dungeon and speak with Adventurer Alard (iz_dun03 34, 167).

Alard will challenge you to a material collection contest. Bring sea materials (Gills: 1 pt, Sharp Scales: 2 pts, Fish Tails: 2 pts, Ancient Lips: 20 pts) to compare scores with him. If you beat his score, he will reward you with a Potion Box.

3 Trident of the Depths

Speak with the Chief Archeologist (iz_dun03 34, 171). He will inform you that breaching the ancient seal requires a key artifact called the Trident of the Depths.

Search the 5 Seaweed patches swaying around the 3rd floor until you find the Trident. Note: You must be in a party. Once obtained, the party leader speaks to the Chief Archeologist to open the gateway and enter the instance.

4 The Inner Cavern Waves

Upon entering the underwater ruins (1@iz_dun03), the Chief Archeologist will warn you that breaking the seal has agitated the ocean depths.

Defeat 2 aggressive waves of sea creatures (Vadon, Obeaune, Marse, Marc, and Swordfish) to secure the camp before the main guardian appears!

5 The Boss fight

The Mermaid of the Depths has emerged from the abyss! Her hypnotic singing voice and water magic resonate through the watery ruins. Defeat her to secure the excavation site!

Mermaid's Tear

Type: Accessory | Required Level: 1 | Slots: 1

A radiant blue gem formed from the sorrowful tears of the Mermaid of the Depths. It resonates with oceanic winds.

Increases physical and magical damage against Fish race monsters by 10%.
Allows the use of Violent Gale Level 3.

⚔️ Boss Skills & Strategy

Skills: Uses Water Element attacks, Water Ball, and Cold Bolt.

Strategy: As a Fish race and Water 2 element monster, Wind Element physical and magical attacks deal massive damage. Equip Water-resistant armor or potions to survive her heavy water spell damage.

6 Instance Rewards & Open World Boss

After defeating the Mermaid of the Depths, report to Archeologist Dan at the dungeon entrance (izlu2dun 114, 88).

Completing the expedition rewards you with 175,000 Base EXP, 210,000 Job EXP, a Proof of Valor, and your choice of 1 ancient headgear: Pisces Crown or Cancer Diadem.

🔁 Repeatable Open World Boss (FFA Mode)

Once the main quest is completed for the first time, you can summon the Mermaid of the Depths again in the open public map of Byalan Dungeon F3 (iz_dun03 34, 171) every 3 hours.

Required Offerings: 30 Fins, 30 Sharp Scales, and 30 Fish Tails.
