📜 Side Quest Guide

1 Wayfarer Lloyd's Request

Locate Wayfarer Lloyd at the Izlude Docks (izlude 130, 70).

He is drafting specialized maps for new adventurers venturing into Midgard. He will offer to craft you a Wayfarer's Map along with 10 Wayfarer's Stones if you supply the following materials for his drafting ink:

🪶 5x Feather
🧪 5x Sticky Mucus
🧪 1x Empty Bottle
💰 1,000 Zeny
🗺️ Quest Item Info

2 Wayfarer's Map

Type: Usable Item (Special) | Weight: 0 | Account Bound

A masterfully crafted map of Midgard provided by Lloyd to guide new adventurers across the realm.

Item Functionality:
Allows the user to cast Warp Portal Level 4 to open portals to saved memo locations. Consumes 1x Wayfarer's Stone per use as fuel. This map is reusable and does not disappear after casting.

🔮 Catalyst Info

3 Wayfarer's Stone

Type: Etc Item / Catalyst | Weight: 10

A smooth stone infused with spatial mana.

Item Functionality:
Acts as the required catalyst fuel to power the teleportation magic within your Wayfarer's Map. You receive 10x Wayfarer's Stones upon finishing the quest.
