Magical Bullet Max Level: 1

Sp Cost: 2% Max SP per activation

Now functions as a toggle support skill. Adds bonus damage to normal attacks equal to 20% of your Max SP. This bonus damage decays as your remaining SP decreases. Automatically toggles off if remaining SP drops below 20%.

Damage Bonus Scaling:
- 100% - 80% SP: Grants 100% bonus damage (20% Max SP as damage)
- 79% - 60% SP: Grants 75% bonus damage (15% Max SP as damage)
- 59% - 40% SP: Grants 50% bonus damage (10% Max SP as damage)
- 39% - 20% SP: Grants 25% bonus damage (5% Max SP as damage)

Cracker Max Level: 1

Sp Cost: 10

Status effect changed from Stun to Confusion. Now deals AoE effect in a 5x5 area centered around the caster.

Tracking Max Level: 10

Sp Cost: 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 / 40 / 45 / 50 / 55 / 60

Retains original mechanics, and can now deal Critical hits. If cast on an enemy under the "Exposed" status, increases final damage by +50%.

Desperado Max Level: 10

Sp Cost: 32 / 34 / 36 / 38 / 40 / 42 / 44 / 46 / 48 / 50

No longer hits a random number of times. Strikes 2 guaranteed hits dealing 100% + (10% * Skill Level) damage to all surrounding enemies in range (hits are no longer split among targets). Every 20 AGI increases hit count by +1. Consumes 6 Bullets base, and each bonus hit gained from AGI consumes 3 additional Bullets.

Dust Max Level: 10

Sp Cost: 3 + 3 * Skill Level

Now attacks in an Area of Effect (AoE) and knocks targets back by 3 cells. Damage ratio adjusted to 100% + (20% * Skill Level). Has a 2-second global cooldown.

Spread Attack Max Level: 10

Sp Cost: 15 + 5 * Skill Level

Increases damage ratio to 100% + (20% * Skill Level).

Ground Drift Max Level: 10

Sp Cost: 4 + 2 * Skill Level

Damage is now influenced by weapon cards. Ignores Flee, Auto Guard, and Cicada Skin Shedding.

Madness Canceller Max Level: 1

Sp Cost: 30

ASPD bonus reduced to +10%, and ATK bonus increased to +150. Duration increased to 30 seconds. Cooldown: 45 seconds. Global delay reduced to 2 seconds.

Soul of the Peacekeeper (NEW) Max Level: 1

Passive / Platinum

Enhances Tracking, Desperado, Gatling Fever, and Madness Canceller:

- Tracking: When cast on an enemy under the "Exposed" status, reduces Casting Time by 50%.

- Desperado: Killing enemies with this skill restores 2% of your Max HP.

- Gatling Fever: Increases ASPD cap to 193. While active alongside Magic Bullet, each attack continuously increases ASPD by +0.1%. Losing either status resets this bonus ASPD.

- Madness Canceller: No longer prevents movement; instead, reduces Movement Speed by 50%. This penalty combines non-additively with Gatling Fever (-66% total penalty if both are active).

Soul of the Enforcer (NEW) Max Level: 1

Passive / Platinum

Enhances Adjustment, Magical Bullet, Dust, Full Buster, Spread Attack, and Ground Drift:

- Adjustment: Also increases Melee Physical Resistance by +20%.

- Magical Bullet: Increases damage dealt by Dust, Full Buster, Spread Attack, and Ground Drift by +25% in exchange for +50% increased SP consumption.

- Dust: Has a 50% chance to inflict "PSlow" status (Wounding Shot effect) for 10 seconds.

- Full Buster: If target is under the "Exposed" status, ignores target Defense.

- Spread Attack: When cast while under "Adjustment", strikes 2 hits.

- Ground Drift: Adds +200% MATK to damage calculation. When used together with Magical Bullet, grenades apply special status effects:
  • Flare Sphere: Burning (50% chance, 20s duration, 100% MATK)
  • Lightning Sphere: Electrocute (50% chance, 20s duration, 100% MATK)
  • Poison Sphere: Poison (50% chance, 20s duration). Attacking Poisoned targets with this skill increases final damage by +30%.
  • Freezing Sphere: Freezing (50% chance, 20s duration)
  • Tremor Sphere (NEW): Buried (50% chance, 20s duration, 100% MATK)
  • Dark Sphere (Blind Sphere): Decay (NEW Shadow DoT) (50% chance, 20s duration, 100% MATK)
  • Light Sphere (NEW): Radiance (NEW Holy DoT) (50% chance, 20s duration, 100% MATK)
