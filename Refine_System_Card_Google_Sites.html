🛡️ System Guide

SYSTEM Equipment Durability Refinement System

Refining Workshop
Locations
📍 Major Cities Prontera, Geffen, Morroc, Payon, Alberta

How does it work?

  • Durability Index (100/100): All refinable equipment (Headgear Top, Armor, Shield, Weapon, Garment, Footgear) comes with 100 Durability.
  • No Level Downgrade: Failing a refine attempt never decreases your refine level (a +8 weapon stays +8 on failure!).
  • Durability Reduction on Failure: When a refine attempt fails, the equipment loses Durability based on its weapon level and target refine level.
  • Breakage at 0 Durability: If an item's Durability drops to 0, the equipment breaks and is destroyed permanently.
  • Safe Refine Levels: Upgrading gear up to its safe limit is 100% guaranteed (Armors & Lv4 Weapons: +4, Lv3 Weapons: +5, Lv2 Weapons: +6, Lv1 Weapons: +7).
  • Weapon Level 4 Cost Discount: Refining Level 4 Weapons costs 10,000 Zeny per attempt (reduced from 20,000 Zeny).
